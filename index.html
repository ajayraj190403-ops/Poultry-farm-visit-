3. Biology and Physiology of the Animal

Reproductive Biology (Hen)

Only the left ovary and left oviduct are functional. Egg progression takes approximately 24 to 26 hours:

  • Infundibulum (15–30 min): Fertilization site and yolk capture.
  • Magnum (2.5–3 hours): Secretion of thick and thin albumen proteins.
  • Isthmus (1–1.5 hours): Formation of inner and outer shell membranes.
  • Uterus (18–20 hours): Deposition of calcium carbonate (CaCO3) and pigment.
Egg Formation Timeline:
[Yolk Release] -> Infundibulum (20 min) -> Magnum (3 hrs) 
-> Isthmus (1.2 hrs) -> Shell Gland (20 hrs) -> Oviposition

4. Housing and Rearing Practices

East-to-West shed orientation minimizes direct solar radiation inside the pens. High overhangs and central ridge ventilation exhaust ammonia and hot air. The farm uses elevated 3-tier "A-frame" battery cages.

Rajasthan Summer Management

High temperatures (>40°C) induce severe heat stress. The farm utilizes high-pressure roof foggers, wet gunny bag curtains, and continuous overhead air circulators.

5. Farm Management & Health

  • Footbaths: Saturated with potassium permanganate (KMnO4) at shed entry points.
  • Vehicle Disinfection: Wheel spray wash at the farm entrance.

Revenue Streams

  1. Commercial Table Eggs: Primary income stream distributed daily to Ajmer and Kishangarh.
  2. Spent Hens: Sold at the end of the 72–76 week laying cycle for meat.
  3. Organic Manure: Dry droppings sold to local mustard farmers.

7. Conclusion

The field visit to Fakhruddin Poultry Farm, Ajmer provided direct insight into the applied principles of zoological sciences, animal nutrition, and large-scale biosecurity management. The operation demonstrates how commercial poultry farming functions efficiently in the semi-arid climate of Rajasthan through appropriate strain selection, climate-controlled housing, and strict health measures.
